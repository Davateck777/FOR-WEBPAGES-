01The Question Bank — what the target audience must get answers to

Videos answer questions, not feature lists. Q1–Q7 are core — every campaign asset answers all seven. Q8–Q12 are the deeper trust-layer questions — the ones whose answers actually move a viewer to onboarding.

Q1 · CORE

"What exactly is TradeStack?"

Answer angle: one line, ≤ 14 words — "one halal-first app to trade crypto, sell gift cards, pay China suppliers and bills."

🎞 Show: the app icon + one dashboard screen. Nothing else.

Q2 · CORE

"Is this for someone like me?"

Answer angle: name them — students & freelancers paid in gift cards, traders who need fast naira exits, importers buying from China, and halal-conscious users who've stayed away entirely.

🎞 Show: people like them, in places like theirs — campus, market, shop.

Q3 · CORE

"Why should I care?"

Answer angle: the category is a scam minefield — used-card errors, vanished admins, payouts "pending since Tuesday." And if you're faith-conscious, you've blocked yourself off entirely. Both problems end here.

🎞 Show: recreated chat screenshots of the classic scam pattern (never real users).

Q4 · CORE

"Why TradeStack, not the exchanger in my DMs?"

Answer angle: verification you can see — every card checked in 3 steps before you commit, real-time rates, instant payouts, "No Scam Zone" on the record.

🎞 Show: the verification flow + a payout timestamp. DM exchangers can't show either.

Q5 · CORE

"Why now?"

Answer angle: forex scarcity makes China payments brutal for importers; category trust is at its lowest; the halal-first position is unclaimed ground. First mover takes the community's trust.

🎞 Show: the community growing — waitlist counter, group activity.

Q6 · CORE

"How does it actually work?"

Answer angle: a 30–60s screen demo, Loom-style: KYC → fund wallet → sell a card / trade crypto → naira credited → withdraw. Timer running. Nothing hidden.

🎞 Show: real flows, real timing, real screens — recorded once, reused everywhere.

Q7 · CORE

"What do I do next?"

Answer angle: ONE CTA only — join the WhatsApp community / early access. Never two doors.

🎞 Show: the WhatsApp group card on screen for the last 3 seconds.

Q7b · RULE

The one-CTA rule

Any asset that ends with "download AND follow AND visit AND join" converts to none of them. Pick the group join; the group does the rest.

🎞 Every script on this page ends with the same door.

Q8 · TRUST

"Is my money safe?"

Answer angle: KYC onboarding, bank-grade encryption, cold storage with multi-sig, traceable wallets — TradeStack's own security posture, shown not claimed.

🎞 Show: the security section of the site/app on screen. Silence while it's read.

Q9 · TRUST

"Is trading here actually halal?"

Answer angle: THE question for segment S4 — and the campaign's first domino. Who advises? What makes crypto/gift-card trading on this platform halal? Placeholder until the governance pack lands.

🎞 Show: named scholars/standards once supplied. This is the single highest-leverage asset in the whole campaign — hold Ep.1 until it's real.

Q10 · TRUST

"What will it cost me?"

Answer angle: the rate you see is the rate you get — real-time, on screen before you commit. "This is everything, there is nothing else."

🎞 Show: the live rates page, held on screen for 5 full seconds.

Q11 · TRUST

"How fast do I get paid?"

Answer angle: "instant / within minutes" is the claim — prove it. A live sell with a timer running in the corner of the screen.

🎞 Show: the withdrawal landing in the bank notification. Timestamp visible.

Q12 · TRUST

"Who else like me is using it?"

Answer angle: real people — a student who sells cards monthly, a Bodija importer, a P2P trader — in their own words, not actors.

🎞 Show: REAL testimonials. Flag: the live site's stock personas ("Sarah Chen — Virtual Lagos") must be replaced before any campaign traffic lands.

FORMAT

Objection-crusher series

Each trust question gets its own short video — one objection per episode, answered on screen (see Frameworks, FW-4). Five episodes = the whole trust layer.

🎞 These five videos will do more for onboarding than any amount of hype.

02Key-point doctrine — six points every asset reinforces

Before any script is approved, it must pass this checklist — all six, no exceptions.

KP1Identity

"TradeStack — the halal-first way to trade crypto & gift cards and pay across borders" (exact wording pending the governance pack).

KP2Differentiator

Verification you can see: 3-step card checks, real-time rates, instant payouts — "No Scam Zone" as a promise with receipts.

KP3Felt pain

Scam fatigue + faith hesitation — the two blocks that keep the audience out (→ pain map).

KP4Proof

Security posture, verification flow, payout timers, real testimonials — shown, never claimed.

KP5Simplicity

One app for five jobs: crypto, cards, China payments, airtime, data, electricity. No app-hopping.

KP6Single CTA

Join the WhatsApp community — one door, every asset, every time.

03Script frameworks by video type

Five frameworks — one per video type. Copy the beat sheet, fill the brackets, route through review, ship.

FW-1PAS · cold-traffic hook ads · 15–30s · TikTok / Reels / WhatsApp status

0–3sPROBLEM — state the pain in their words. No logo. Direct gaze or bold text.
3–10sAGITATE — make the hidden cost visible and personal.
10–22sSOLUTION — TradeStack + the differentiator (KP2).
22–27sPROOF — receipts on screen (KP4).
27–30sCTA — one door: the WhatsApp community.
Example · P1 draft HOOK: "You send the gift card first. Then… silence."
AGITATE: "No receipt. No payout. Admin 'last seen' two days ago. You know this story — or you know somebody it happened to."
SOLUTION: "TradeStack verifies every card in 3 steps before you commit — and settles to your naira wallet in minutes."
PROOF: Verification screens. Payout timestamp. No scam zone — on the record.
CTA: "Link below."

FW-2BAB · feature demos · 30–45s

0–5sBEFORE — the world as it is (the DM exchanger, the pending payout, the forex runaround).
5–12sAFTER — the same scene, transformed. Feel it before you explain it.
12–35sBRIDGE — the app demo that gets them there (Q6 flow).
35–45sCTA — one door.
Example · P5 draft · importer version BEFORE: "Your supplier in Guangzhou wants RMB today. Your bank wants forms, patience, and a rate that hurts."
AFTER: "Naira in. RMB paid to your supplier. Up to 30% saved on the preorder."
BRIDGE: [20s screen demo of the ₦→RMB payment flow].
CTA: "Bring your shipment. Link below."

20–45sDESIRE — the felt pain (Q3) + the differentiator (KP2) + receipts (KP4).
45–60sACTION — the door, again.

20–35sRECEIPTS — the actual security page / rates page / payout record, held long enough to read.
35–40sCTA — "Full walkthrough in the community."

FW-5Testimonial story arc · 45–60s · card-seller & importer versions

Pilot castingCard-seller version: a student who sells cards monthly. Importer version: a Bodija / Gbagi trader buying from China. Real people, release forms signed. Replaces the site's stock personas.

RULESBeat-sheet rules — all videos

  • First 3 seconds carry the pain — never the logo, never the intro.
  • Subtitles always on — most viewing is sound-off; localise (Yoruba / Hausa / Pidgin) where the placement calls for it.
  • ≤ 40 words on screen at any moment.
  • Receipts beat adjectives — a payout timestamp outperforms the word "trusted."
  • One CTA — the WhatsApp community (KP6).
  • No "halal" claims in copy until the governance pack clears — positioning stays factual until then.
  • Rate & payout claims must match live reality — record on the day, timestamp it.
  • Every published link is UTM-tagged (→ convention).
